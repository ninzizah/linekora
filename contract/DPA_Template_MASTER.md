## 1. PURPOSE

This Data Protection Agreement ("DPA") sets out the terms that apply when Personal Data is processed by the Processor on behalf of the Data Controller in the course of providing services. This DPA supplements and forms part of the main service agreement between the parties.

This DPA is required for compliance with the **Rwanda Data Protection Law No. 3/2021** and the **Personal Data Protection Policy (PDPB)** issued by the Rwanda Data Protection Authority (DPA).

## 3. SCOPE OF PROCESSING

### 3.1 Data Categories Processed

The Processor shall process the following categories of Personal Data on behalf of the Controller:

| Data Category | Data Elements | Purpose |
|---------------|---------------|---------|
| Worker PII | Name, Phone, Gender, District, Sector, Village, ID, Photo, Skills | Registration, KYC, Task Matching |
| Employer PII | Name, Phone, Company, Location, Payment Details | Registration, Task Creation, Payments |
| User Activity Data | IP Address, Device ID, Geolocation | Security, Analytics |
| Payment Data | Card Details, Billing Info, Transaction ID | Payment Processing |
| Notification Data | Name, Phone, Transaction ID | SMS/Mobile Money Alerts |
| Support Data | Name, Phone, Issue Description | Customer Support |
| Auth Data | UID, Phone, Role, Auth Token | Authentication, Session Management |

### 3.2 Purpose of Processing

The Processor shall process Personal Data solely for the purpose of providing the agreed services to the Controller, and not for any other purpose without the Controller's prior written consent.

### 3.3 Duration of Processing

This DPA shall remain in effect for the duration of the main service agreement, and for a period of **2 years** after termination (for data retention obligations).

---

## 4. OBLIGATIONS OF THE PROCESSOR

### 4.1 Processing Instructions

The Processor shall:
- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Immediately inform the Controller if an instruction infringes on data protection laws
- Not process Personal Data for any purpose other than the agreed services

### 4.2 Confidentiality

The Processor shall ensure that:
- All persons authorized to process Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Access to Personal Data is limited to authorized personnel only
- Personal Data is not disclosed to unauthorized third parties

### 4.3 Security Measures

The Processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including but not limited to:

- [ ] **Encryption** — AES-256 encryption at rest; TLS 1.2+ in transit
- [ ] **Access Controls** — Role-based access control (RBAC)
- [ ] **Audit Logging** — All access to Personal Data is logged
- [ ] **Vulnerability Management** — Regular security assessments
- [ ] **Incident Response** — Documented breach notification procedures
- [ ] **Data Minimization** — Only collect/process data necessary for the purpose

### 4.4 Sub-Processors

The Processor shall:
- Obtain prior written authorization from the Controller before engaging sub-processors
- Maintain a current list of all sub-processors
- Enter into binding agreements with sub-processors that impose equivalent data protection obligations
- Remain fully liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors

### 4.5 Data Subject Rights

The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to Data Subject requests, including:
- Right of access
- Right to rectification
- Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Right to restrict processing
- Right to data portability
- Right to object to processing

### 4.6 Data Breach Notification

The Processor shall:
- Notify the Controller **within 48 hours** of becoming aware of a Personal Data breach
- Provide all information necessary for the Controller to comply with the **72-hour breach notification** requirement under Rwanda's PDPB
- Take immediate steps to contain and remediate the breach

### 4.7 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

The Processor shall cooperate with and assist the Controller in conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments as required under Rwanda's data protection law.

## 6. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

### 6.1 Retention Period

| Data Category | Retention Period | Deletion Method |
|---------------|------------------|-----------------|
| Worker PII | Active account + 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Employer PII | Active account + 2 years | Secure deletion |
| Transaction Records | 7 years (legal requirement) | Archive then delete |
| Support Tickets | Resolution + 1 year | Secure deletion |
| Analytics Data | 1 year | Automatic purge |

### 6.2 Return and Deletion

Upon termination of the service agreement or upon Controller's request, the Processor shall:
- Return all Personal Data to the Controller in a commonly used, machine-readable format
- Delete all copies of Personal Data within **30 days** of receiving the request
- Provide written certification of deletion upon request

---

## 7. AUDIT RIGHTS

The Controller (or its authorized auditor) shall have the right to:
- Conduct audits of the Processor's data processing activities
- Request documentation and evidence of compliance
- Inspect security measures and facilities
- Conduct audits **once per year** or upon reasonable suspicion of non-compliance

The Processor shall cooperate fully with any audit and provide access to relevant records, facilities, and personnel.

---

## 8. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

### 8.1 Liability

The Processor shall be liable for any damage caused by processing that does not comply with this DPA or applicable data protection law.

### 8.2 Indemnification

The Processor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Controller from and against any claims, damages, losses, and expenses arising from the Processor's breach of this DPA.

---

## 9. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 9.1 Term

This DPA shall commence on the Effective Date and remain in effect for the duration of the main service agreement.

### 9.2 Termination

This DPA shall automatically terminate upon:
- Termination of the main service agreement
- Mutual written agreement of the parties
- Breach of material terms that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice

### 9.3 Survival

The obligations under Sections 4 (Confidentiality), 6 (Data Retention), 8 (Liability) shall survive termination of this DPA.

---

## 10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

### 10.1 Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the **Republic of Rwanda**, including:
- Law No. 3/2021 on the Protection of Personal Data
- Personal Data Protection Policy (PDPB) No. 3/2021
- Any regulations issued by the Rwanda Data Protection Authority

### 10.2 Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this DPA shall be resolved through:
1. Good faith negotiation between the parties
2. Mediation
3. Arbitration under the rules of the Kigali International Arbitration Centre
